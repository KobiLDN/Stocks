What this is: stocks where something that could move the price a lot has not been decided yet: a leadership fight, a takeover bid, a regulator's or court's decision, a trial readout, results after a big run. What it isn't: a forecast of which way a stock will go. Nobody outside the room knows that. The odds are the AI's estimate, and every first flag is scored after 30 days on the Track Record, so you can see whether to trust them. Not financial advice.
